# Workshop Cupcake Store

This is fictional training data. It is not a real store, offer or order system.

## Opening hours

The demonstration store is open Monday to Friday, 09:00-17:00.
It is closed on weekends. All times are in the store's local time zone.

## Pickup and delivery

This demonstration supports pickup only. Delivery is not offered.
Questions about inventory or an individual order must use the approved store
MCP service; this FAQ contains no live stock or customer records.

## Returns

For the workshop scenario, damaged items can be reported within 24 hours of
pickup. The assistant may explain the policy but cannot approve a refund.

## Missing information

The FAQ does not specify prices, allergens, payment details or loyalty benefits.
Do not infer those facts. Refer the question to the store operator.
